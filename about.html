Who We Are

A Dedicated Partner for Public-Sector & Commercial Missions

Shaw Global Ventures Inc. is a veteran-led business focused on providing digital communication services and supply solutions to public-sector and commercial clients.

Through GES Social Media Management, we support organizations with strategic social media management, content development, audience engagement, and public awareness initiatives. We also seek to support recurring procurement needs for medical supplies, personal protective equipment, and janitorial products.

Our approach is built around understanding client requirements, communicating clearly, and delivering services and products in accordance with agreed specifications.

Understanding Client Requirements

2

3

Delivering to Agreed Specifications

Strict quality assurance, reliable fulfillment timelines, and compliance with all contractual standards.
